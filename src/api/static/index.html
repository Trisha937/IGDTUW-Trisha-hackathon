MARKET SURVEILLANCE & REBALANCING PARADIGM

The Industry Bottleneck vs. Dynamic Tilting

Traditional quantitative indices suffer from rigid reconstitution schedules, leaving portfolios vulnerable to unforeseen corporate events and regulatory shocks.

× Traditional Quarterly Rebalancing Bottlenecks
  • Information Lag: Material events (e.g. RBI fines, CEO departures) take weeks or months to reflect in quarterly rebalancing schedules.
  • Drawdown Exposure: Funds hold full benchmark weights in distressed securities while news breaks across unstructured channels.
  • Noise vs. Substance: Manual analysts are flooded with thousands of noisy headlines per day, resulting in analysis paralysis.
✓ Our Automated AI/NLP Dynamic Rebalancer
  • Continuous Signal Ingestion: Real-time surveillance of Economic Times RSS, NSE corporate filings, and social chatter.
  • 7-Day Decaying Vector Memory: Retains past context with exponential decay (e−λΔt) so one-off rumors fade smoothly.
  • Strict Bounded Simplex: Tilts weights away from risk and toward catalysts while capping turnover at ≤10% and movement at ±2%.

QUANTITATIVE SAFEGUARDS

Strict Mathematical Bounds & Regulatory Compliance

To prevent erratic whipsawing and maintain institutional risk standards, the optimizer enforces 4 mathematical safety rails at every cycle.

RULE 01
Exact Simplex Normalization
Σ wᵢ = 100.00%
All constituent weights sum exactly to 1.0000. No leverage, cash drag, or unallocated reserves are permitted.
RULE 02
Constituent Box Bounds
5.0% ≤ wᵢ ≤ 20.0%
Hard 5% floor prevents complete liquidation of any stock. 20% ceiling prevents single-stock concentration blowups.
RULE 03
Single-Cycle Movement Cap
|Δwᵢ| ≤ ±2.00%
Dampens sudden sentiment volatility. No stock weight can move by more than 2 percentage points in a single cycle.
RULE 04
Turnover Cost Ceiling
0.5 × Σ|Δwᵢ| ≤ 10.0%
Controls transaction slippage and broker execution costs. Rebalances are rejected or scaled if turnover exceeds 10%.

Quantitative Index Rebalancing Methodology (Module A)

1. Multi-Source Credibility Weighted Sentiment

Incoming signals are aggregated per constituent stock using source credibility coefficients: Official Regulatory Announcements (1.0) > Financial News Feeds (0.8) > Social Chatter (0.3). Social-only aggregate sentiment is constrained to ±0.3 to prevent irrational market noise amplification.

2. Exponential Tilting Allocation Formula

Target constituent weights are calculated proportionally via exponential tilting:
Target Weight w̃ᵢ ∝ 0.10 × exp(0.5 × Sᵢ) where Sᵢ is the net sentiment score [−1.0, +1.0]. Symmetric formulation guarantees non-negativity and market-wide neutrality.

3. Hard Institutional Constraints & Water-Filling Simplex Projection

  • Sum Constraint: ∑ wᵢ = 1.0 (Strictly 100.00% total allocation).
  • Constituent Bounds: 5.0% ≤ wᵢ ≤ 20.0% (Diversification floor and single-stock concentration ceiling).
  • Cycle Movement Limit: |wᵢᵔ⁻¹⁾ − wᵢᵔ| ≤ 2.0% (Smooth, non-erratic portfolio transitions).
  • Turnover Constraint: 0.5 × ∑ |Δwᵢ| ≤ 10.0% (Strict transaction cost control).
